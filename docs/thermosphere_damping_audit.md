# Thermospheric damping audit

The repaired six-hour ERA5 snapshot remains a numerical diagnostic, not a validated whole-atmosphere simulation. `scripts/audit_thermosphere_snapshot.py` opens only the closed snapshot and writes `data/thermosphere_snapshot_audit.h5`. Peak speeds at 287.5 and 487.5 km are 542.9 and 651.8 m/s. Those are total speed maxima, not vertical-only maxima or wave amplitudes after separating the mean flow.

The reference kinematic viscosity is 1.58e6 m2/s at 287.5 km and 5.93e7 m2/s at 487.5 km. A local uniform shear-wave estimate with 50 km vertical wavelength gives amplitude decay times of about 40 s and 1.07 s respectively. This estimate is not a stratified acoustic/gravity-wave attenuation solution, particularly when continuum assumptions fail.

`tests/transport_shortwave_audit.rs` probes an alternating vertical cell pattern in a uniform, closed 64-layer grid, using the actual CPU transport operator. At nu=1e5 m2/s and dz=1 km, it retains 0.881752 of its projected velocity amplitude after 100 s. A scalar nearest-face backward-Euler reference retains 2.868e-10. The centered least-squares gradient and its adjoint have a near-null response to this pattern in the interior. Passing the audit test only confirms diagnostic execution; it does not certify physical damping. Evidence: `data/transport_shortwave_audit.h5`. A coercive, face-based treatment needs conservation, entropy/energy, uniform-flow and spectral-damping regression checks before replacement.

Initialization also extends the 50-km pressure/density multiplier unchanged to the model top while setting winds to zero above the blend. Initial upper density ratios range from 0.615 to 1.316 at every sampled upper altitude. This creates horizontal pressure gradients without a dynamically balanced upper wind. It is an artificial adjustment source to isolate with a horizontally uniform upper control.

Vadas and Fritts (2005) derive gravity-wave dissipation with molecular viscosity and thermal diffusivity: https://doi.org/10.1029/2004JD005574 . Becker and Vadas (2020) use molecular diffusion, Richardson-number-dependent Smagorinsky diffusion, weak hyperdiffusion near spectral truncation, ion drag, radiative physics and consistent thermodynamics; their model top is about 450 km: https://doi.org/10.1029/2020JA028034 . Becker et al. (2022) restrict reanalysis nudging to large scales in the lower atmosphere: https://doi.org/10.1029/2021JD035018 . Becker and Vadas also modify/cap vertical molecular viscosity as rarefied effects become important; simply increasing isotropic diffusion indefinitely is not their prescription.

Current source gaps: no instability-dependent turbulent closure or neutral ion drag, prescribed rather than evolved molecular coefficients, no radiative cooling, a sponge beginning only at 750 km, and continuum propagation to 1000 km without a wavelength-dependent Knudsen check. The existing temperature gate prevents the prior thermal runaway but does not validate velocity amplitudes, wave damping, or the upper-domain physics. At the initial audit stage, no production source had been changed and no replacement run had been launched. The subsequent implementation is recorded below.

## Implemented correction

The diffusion operator retains the Newtonian-stress least-squares energy form and adds an affine-exact residual for every neighbouring pair: the cell jump minus the reconstructed gradient dotted with the centre displacement. The same residual and exact transpose enter the implicit matrix, its diagonal preconditioner, and viscous-work accounting. Constants and affine fields have zero residual; unresolved alternating patterns do not. This is numerical stabilization, not a wave-breaking turbulence parameterization. The uniform one-dimensional scalar interior reduces to nearest-face diffusion when combined with the centered gradient; boundary geometry and spherical curvature remain distinct.

The corrected checkerboard audit retains 0.00143921 amplitude after 100 s. Tests preserve uniform translation, total momentum and energy, and conversion of lost kinetic energy to heat. The evolved acoustic amplitude ratio is 0.844943 versus the local viscous/conductive prediction 0.832280; thermal long-wave amplitude is 0.954915 versus 0.952951. A five-wavelength thermal sweep on refinement level 2 passes an absolute-amplitude error of 0.025 against nearest-face backward-Euler diffusion. The extremely coarse level-0 spherical test differs by about 0.049 for a 32-km mode; that diagnostic is retained in the log rather than interpreted as a plane-wave convergence test. Evidence: `data/transport_face_spectral_validation.h5`, `data/transport_shortwave_corrected_validation.h5`, and `data/molecular_transport_face_mpi_validation.h5`.

The optional `--era5-uniform-upper` control preserves the original weather below 50 km, smoothly removes its extrapolated upper pressure multiplier through 120 km, and restores uniform reference density, temperature and zero velocity above that altitude. The join region can still adjust; this does not prove full geostrophic or hydrostatic balance of the lower weather. Tests enforce bitwise unchanged lower weather and exact upper-reference recovery. The 450-km input is a separate cropped control, not replacement of the archived 1000-km input. A matched 600-s CPU/Metal evolution passes with maximum relative L2 difference 1.24511e-6.

The superseded repaired 24-hour run was stopped before completion. The six-hour 450-km Metal control in `output/era5_face_upper_metal6h` completed, with the temperature gate and unchanged solar prescription. It still reached 652 m/s radial speed near 387.5 km. The matched no-solar control reached 646 m/s there, while a six-hour resting reference remained exactly unchanged. These controls implicate imported-state adjustment rather than the solar source as the immediate cause. No full 24-hour physical-validation claim is made. Smagorinsky wave-breaking closure, ion drag, radiative cooling and a stratified gravity-wave attenuation benchmark remain subsequent physical-validation work.


## Hydrostatic columns

The uniform-upper pressure blend was not vertically balanced. For p=p0 exp(w ln r), hydrostatic density is rho=rho0 exp(w ln r) - p w' ln r/g. The optional analytical join implements this relation. Its initial join acceleration fell from 0.6676 to 0.1240 m/s2; after six hours the 387.5-km radial peak fell from 652 to 298 m/s. That remaining discrete error is not an acceptable resting initialization.

A density-only fixed-pressure projection was tried against the nonlinear MUSCL operator. It did not converge to the requested tolerance and was not used for an evolution run. Logs are `output/era5_discrete_hydrostatic_initial*.log`; the discarded Newton prototype is retained only under `tmp/hydrostatic_projection_failed/`. No failed initialization is marked as validated.

The new `--era5-hydrostatic-columns` path uses ERA5 temperatures below 30 km and a temperature join to MSIS by 50 km. It integrates each column from the first-centre ERA5 pressure:

    ln(p[k]/p[k-1]) = -0.5 * (g[k]/(R[k]*T[k]) + g[k-1]/(R[k-1]*T[k-1])) * (z[k]-z[k-1])
    rho[k] = p[k]/(R[k]*T[k])

Density is derived from this pressure and temperature, rather than independently interpolated. Horizontal ERA5 winds taper to zero between 30 and 50 km; initial vertical wind is zero. These columns establish hydrostatic balance, not geostrophic balance of the imported winds.

Vertical MUSCL and HLLE states use the column reference. Horizontal reconstruction and fluxes retain the common altitude reference and physical weather pressure gradients. The radial geometric reference-pressure contribution is evaluated once with the same CPU flux operator and subtracted, so the wind-free column reference has zero radial pressure/gravity residual. The horizontal pressure gradient is retained. Metal packs separate horizontal and vertical reference states and reconstructs the corresponding fields; CPU transport and energy bookkeeping remain in Rust. The earlier column prototype also changed horizontal reference mapping and is superseded; its six-hour output must not be treated as the corrected atmospheric result.

`tests/hydrostatic_columns.rs` enforces the pressure integration identity, positive thermodynamics, radial balance and retained horizontal forcing. The CPU radial acceleration residual is 4.44e-17 m/s2; the horizontal acceleration is nonzero (0.0266 m/s2). The required Metal comparison passes, as do the existing CPU/Metal flux, gravitational-energy and temperature tests. This is an operator test, not a claim of a steady three-dimensional weather state.

The frozen v2 source and binary are in `output/hydrostatic_columns_v2_frozen_sources/`, with `manifest.sha256`. Matched CPU/Metal/two-rank 600-s controls and a six-hour Metal evolution are executed by `tmp/run_hydrostatic_columns_controls_v2.sh`. They use the separate 450-km input and no artificial velocity or temperature clipping. `scripts/validate_hydrostatic_columns.py` requires closure, the v2 scheme marker, correct initial pressure/density, the hydrostatic integration identity, positive finite evolved fields, and the exact requested end. `scripts/export_viewer_layer.py` now subtracts the actual column pressure reference when present. No new 24-hour run has been accepted yet.


The final column initializer additionally matches a common upper pressure by 120 km. It adjusts inverse temperature smoothly only in 50-120 km, with a nonnegative shape function that vanishes at both ends, so the integrated hydrostatic pressure reaches the chosen common anchor. Pressure and density are identical between columns above that join, avoiding the inherited lower-atmosphere pressure contrast driving an unbalanced upper wind. ERA5 temperatures below 30 km are retained; 30-50 km joins to MSIS. All resulting inverse temperatures must be positive. `hydrostatic_column_initialization=3` identifies this version. It uses the corrected horizontal/vertical flux scheme (`hydrostatic_column_flux_scheme=2`).

The final sources are frozen in `output/hydrostatic_columns_v3_frozen_sources/`. `scripts/plot_hydrostatic_initialization.py` makes `output/thermosphere_audit/hydrostatic_initialization.png/.pdf` from the closed 600-s CPU control, with source and plot hashes in its HDF5 sidecar. The final CPU/Metal 600-s relative L2 difference is 2.19551e-6; the pressure integration residual is 6.0e-15 in log pressure. Evolution with transport, solar heating and imported horizontal winds still requires inspection; hydrostatic balance is not geostrophic balance or full thermospheric validation. No turbulence, ion drag or radiative loss was silently added.

### Closed v3 six-hour energy audit

The final hydrostatic-column control completed 21,600 s (37 saved frames). Pressure integration residual was 5.9952e-15; finite/positive-state and molecular-transport checks passed. At 387.5 km the final maximum radial speed was 157.3 m/s and temperature 1088.9 K. This is a numerical diagnostic, not a validated atmospheric forecast; imported horizontal winds are not geostrophically balanced.

`scripts/audit_era5_energy.py` inventories the closed states in `data/era5_columns_v3_energy_audit.h5`. ERA5 is loaded at initialization only. Initial kinetic energy is 8.47996e20 J. Over six hours, gas plus gravitational potential energy increases by 7.496543e20 J, while the sampled prescribed solar input is 7.496509e20 J (difference about 4.6 parts per million of the solar contribution). Sampled sponge gas-energy exchange is -1.48266e14 J. This is a sampled diagnostic, not an exact discrete budget: it omits explicit boundary flux and sponge potential-energy exchange. The close agreement identifies the solar source as the continuing net input in this control. The prescribed 20% absorption of 1361 W/m2 has no compensating radiative cooling. Initial imbalance can convert stored energy into motion, but ERA5 itself is not a repeated energy injection. The earlier matched solar-off control also had strong upper radial motion, so solar heating alone does not explain the prior failure.
